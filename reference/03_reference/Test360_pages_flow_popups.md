# Vedantu Test360: landing pages, exact flows, pop-ups, and what to add

Sources: Vedans's own chats and canvases (pop-ups canvas "Test360 upgrade pop-ups – JEE, BITSAT, NDA", Limits Mini Test, Test360 test flow, Test360 pages v2), and his Oct 19 whiteboard flows. Nothing below is new invention unless it is under "What we can add".

## RULES THAT HOLD FOR EVERY PAGE
1. Vedantu-native look: orange (#FF6B35 / #FE6838) + navy (#141B4D). Purple is only for Ved AI things (ScoreBooster, analysis, AI correction), never to sell a product.
2. JEE and Tamil Nadu State Board (TNSB) are two fully separate products, each with its own entry and student flow. Not one stitched site.
3. Everything is open: all 5 levels of every topic, no locks, no "Coming soon", no daily cap. Students binge. The paid layer sits after the free practice.
4. Chapter-wise tests only. No full mocks, no rank or percentile, no promise of live sessions, and (per Vedans) no video-solutions promise on the series.
5. The test screen is a clone of the Eklavya JEE Main test (look AND questions).
6. Ved ScoreBooster / VedAI dashboard is a headline selling point, not buried at the bottom.
7. Free product must show quantified proof of improvement (before and after).
8. Templatized: the same pages copy-paste to other exams.

## B. EXACT FLOW: JEE (as Vedans gave it)
JEE page > free test > free-test page with every chapter divided by Physics / Chemistry / Maths > chapter > topic summary page (double-click option) > pick topics and level of topic > give test > result + analysis > upsell / what to explore next (same topic higher level, or next topic).

Details he fixed:
- **Topic summary:** double-clicking sections of the summary opens the full version; single tap = short. (Also add a visible "Full detail" button because double-click is not discoverable on a phone.)
- **Pick topics and level:** all levels open; student chooses total question count, default 3 per picked topic, adjustable up or down.
- **Test:** Eklavya format (instructions, section tabs, Question Type bar, +4/-1, palette, timer, Exam Summary, "Are you sure", success).
- **Result + analysis:** Ved ScoreBooster card (purple), Easy Kill Topics, skipped questions, weakest topic, "How to improve" and the pop-up.
- **Journey Screens stages to keep:** Video > Summary > Test > Result > Pop-up > Fix > Plans > Retest > Progress.
- **Gamification (his rule):** chapter > topics > Levels 1-5; Level 5 is mastery; overall points; a student who fails a level can go another round.

## C. EXACT FLOW: TAMIL NADU STATE BOARD (12th first, then 11th)
Same chapter-wise page, same topic summaries, same pick-topic. After the test: **solutions + the "upsell AI correction"**, then the same upsell as JEE.
- All questions are subjective (no MCQ). No detailed post-test report.
- Seven-plus subjects: Physics, Maths, Chemistry, Tamil, Computer Science, Bio-Botany, Bio-Zoology.
- Earlier decision in another chat (still open): self-marking against the examiner's scheme, with advanced schemes locked as the upsell, and teacher-checked answer sheets as the series differentiator. Which of these sits next to the AI correction is not decided.

## D. THE POP-UPS (the most important selling layer)
**1. JEE post-result pop-up (done).** Over the blurred result page. Chip "Your Limits report is ready". Headline "You can win back 18 marks". Line: "Start with 0/0 forms, where you lost the most." Two cards: Limits chapter pack Rs 99 (chapter-wise tests, revision notes, concept videos) and Complete JEE Main series Rs 2,999 (200 full-syllabus + 200 half-syllabus tests, notes and videos for all 90 chapters, Ved ScoreBooster analysis; the dark highlighted card). Links: "Fix it free first" (leads to the Fix stage) and "Not now".
**2. BITSAT pop-up (done).** Headline "Writing JEE? Try a free BITSAT test too". Subtext: same report and fixes you just saw, built for BITSAT. Three cards: Free BITSAT chapter test (Try first), BITSAT test series Rs 599, All Engineering Exams combo Rs 2,999 (Best value, dark card). It asks them to try the exam, it does NOT promise JEE marks.
**3. NDA pop-up (done).** Headline "Writing JEE? Try a free NDA test too". Two cards: Free NDA chapter test, NDA test series Rs 499 (Maths and GAT mocks). NDA cadet image. No middle tier.
**4. Mini Test pop-ups (done, bottom sheets):** pop-up A (test series) and pop-up B (notes + textbook), then payment, bought/success, and the failed state.
**5. Other pop-ups on the canvas:** exam picker, exit-intent, save-report, plus 11 pop-up types each for BITSAT and NDA with 2 desktop and 2 mobile designs each. I could not re-read every name; copy the exact list from the canvas.
**6. TNSB pop-ups (needed):** after solutions, the AI correction pop-up (Ved purple), then the shared upsell pop-up. A Class 12 digital-textbook pop-up for TN, since class 11/12 students are funnelled to the textbook as well as the series.
**Rules for pop-ups:** each is 1 headline, 2-3 cards, one dark "best value" card, a free path always visible, "Not now" always visible; desktop AND mobile versions; the pop-up price ladder must come from ONE source so pages never disagree.

## E. WHAT WE CAN ADD (engagement and conversion)
Each item names its source.
1. **One price sheet feeding every page** (one row per exam/series/pack: price, contents, free level, cross-sell targets). Stops the Rs 99 / 299 / 2,999 / 6,000 drift. Source: the "master product sheet" proposed in his earlier chats.
2. **Pop-up trigger map** (when each pop-up appears): after result (always), exit-intent, after Level 3 cleared, after a streak day 3, chapter-complete, return visit. Source: Hacking Growth (trigger-timed prompts).
3. **Quick win in the first 2 minutes**: free Level 1 of a topic that ends with a visible score gain. Source: Hacking Growth (shorten time to the "aha").
4. **Proof of improvement panel** (before/after chart on every result and progress page; the conversion thesis).
5. **Streak + points + titles, with streak-freeze**; a daily mission is a bonus, not a cap.
6. **Level-up and badge moments** (confetti only for good results), with a "what unlocks next" teaser.
7. **Referral**: Rs 100 pack free if a friend completes a test.
8. **Chapter map as a Bento grid**: pastel topic tiles with progress rings under an orange hero card (the direction he picked).
9. **Save-my-report capture** (phone number or email) after the result, to retarget with the pop-ups.
10. **Teacher callback CTA** on the state-board flow, since no report exists there.
11. **Stack the value before the price** on the plans pop-up (Hormozi: show what is included, then the price).
12. **Language switch** (English / Tamil) on TNSB pages and the Tamil hook slip in the summary.
